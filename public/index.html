How it works

Six steps from Australia to your door in Nigeria

  1. 1

    Cargo pickup across Australia

    We collect your goods from wherever you are in Australia.

  2. 2

    Documents checked

    Your paperwork is reviewed before your cargo moves.

    • Commercial invoice
    • Packing list
    • Bill of Lading / Air Waybill
    • HS codes
    • Permits
  3. 3

    Air or sea freight

    Your cargo travels by air or sea, depending on the method you choose.

  4. 4

    Nigerian customs

    Customs clearance in Nigeria, with documentation and duty & tax advice.

  5. 5

    Cargo release

    Once cleared, your cargo is released for collection or delivery.

  6. 6

    Delivery to door

    Door-to-door delivery to your chosen address in Nigeria.
